# Independent source review · 5 September 2026

Three subagents reviewed the four existing reliefs against the original atlas images and the supplied dissertation pages. Each reviewed models outside its original tracing assignment. The site owner inspected the proposed overlays and revised geometry before integrating the changes.

This review concerns fidelity to the published drawing. It does not certify the archaeological interpretation of every mark, provide a new survey, or establish building elevations. The original source images and their checksums remain unchanged.

## Pautalia · Fig. 50

- Added 15 individually visible wall-attached support marks: eleven along the room-3 partition, three at its southern boundary and one beside the short return in room 4. The support layer now contains 140 observed marks. No missing row is completed.
- Removed the three raised semicircular profiles labelled `a` in the northern strip. The supplied plan, text and Fig. 51 do not establish them as horizontal footprints; they remain visible in the source underlay.
- Checked the hypocaust against the author's photograph in Fig. 49 and the two sections in Fig. 51. These show openings and different vertical arrangements. They do not support turning all of the plan's alignments into solid walls, imposing a uniform column type, or reconstructing the whole arcade system.
- Replaced the seven broad arcade bands with narrow copper alignment markers. Added the three readable open strokes of the northern staircase and corrected its displaced cap. Marker widths are display conventions; no arch opening, pier spacing or stair elevation is synthesized.

Printed p. 91 corroborates wall-adjacent pilae but does not provide complete heights for the individually drawn supports. The source's empty or unexcavated areas remain empty.

## Diocletianopolis · Fig. 37

- Restored fourteen clearly drawn white aperture marks that the original minimum-area filter had filled. The eight masonry polygons now retain fifteen holes.
- Removed two thin ink spurs extending into the western excavation hatching and a patterned southwest strip that had been included by the solid-black trace.
- Retained the five named rooms, source openings and separate black masonry components. The disconnected southern continuation remains outside the model's stated scope.

The model is the Momina salza thermal complex. The separate Toplitsa bath in Fig. 15, other spring structures and modern additions must not be combined with this footprint.

Photographs in Figs. 28, 29 and 63 constrain the reading of east-side installations, the caldarium-2 recess and pipes embedded in masonry. They were not registered to the plan, so no footprint coordinates or heights were extracted from them. The review also corrected the old “0.8 pixel” accuracy claim: that number described contour simplification, not source interpretation. Estimated reading/sampling tolerance is approximately ±2 raster pixels.

## Starozagorski Bani · Fig. 24

- Corrected the detached catchment-column footprints to follow the size and rotation of the source's black squares.
- Reviewed each coloured basin identifier against its innermost drawn boundary. The colouring identifies plan areas; it supplies no water surface, basin depth or stair elevation.
- Restored the separate square mark in the semicircular feature adjoining catchment 6. Corrected the arbitrary pool-5 inset, the northern interruption at pool 1 and smaller pool-2 boundary errors; copied the readable basin-edge lines as low copper markers.
- Extended the review to the previously omitted northern enclosure and spaces 9–14. Their hatched bands have readable paired edges and can be traced without inventing wall thickness. They are shown separately in sage; the drawing provides no explicit phase key, so the colour identifies its convention rather than a historical date.

Structure 6 remains identified as an underground catchment, with no invented relationship in elevation to the bath. Feature 7 is a channel, not a room; dashed routes remain in the source image. Room interiors, doorway interruptions and the rounded end of space 13 remain open, with no double thickness at shared black/hatched boundaries.

## Mineralni Bani (Haskovo) · Fig. 19

Reviewed the two second-group basin outlines, visible step lines, open outer boundaries and northern rectangles against the full dissertation plate. Hatching remains unfilled and the underground drainage/catchment lines remain unraised. The first-group pool and stone spout are separate evidence and cannot be transferred into this model.

The traced geometry already matches this reproduction within its stated tolerance, so it is retained. Feature notes now make the source discrepancy explicit: Table 2 gives both basins the same dimensions, while the drawn proportions differ. The model follows the plate without silently resizing either basin to fit the table.
